# Privacy Policy for VSA Simulator

**Last updated: 7 November 2026**

VSA Simulator is made by Vidyasagar Academy (developed by Prof. Dattaraj S. Vidyasagar and Prof. Yash D. Vidyasagar). It is free software under the GNU General Public License v3.0 and runs entirely on your own computer.

## What VSA Simulator does with your data

- **It does not collect, store, send or share any personal information.**
- It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
- It does not connect to the internet by itself. The circuits, programs and firmware files you open or save stay on your computer.
- Your preferences (grid, wire style and similar) and the list of recent projects are saved on your own computer only, in the application's data folder. You can delete them at any time.
- It asks for no access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or other personal data.

## Links

The website, GitHub and e-mail links in the About box, the User Guide and the source-code window open your own web browser or mail program only when you click them. That visit is outside the app and follows the privacy policy of the website you visit. VSA Simulator opens only the addresses of Vidyasagar Academy (https://vsa.edu.in/ and https://github.com/dsvakola) and the support e-mail address.

## Children

The software is educational and is suitable for all ages. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
